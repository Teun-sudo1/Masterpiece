Masterpiece

Public-domain paintings from great museums, fitted to your screens.

  1. Browse. Pick museums, a subject or period, or search for an artist.
  2. Select. Click + on the paintings you like. Click a picture to see it larger.
  3. Set as wallpaper. Choose how below, then click the button in the bottom right.
Masterpiece rotation: a new painting at the interval you choose, fitted to each screen.
Windows slideshow: Windows shuffles them itself and crops them to fit.
Just once: set it now and leave it.

Paintings are saved in Pictures\Masterpiece. Stop the rotation any time with stop at the bottom of the app.
An independent open-source project, not an official app of any museum.
