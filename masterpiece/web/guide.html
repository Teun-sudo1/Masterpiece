Masterpiece

Public-domain paintings from great museums, fitted to your screens.

  1. Search art. Find paintings by artist, subject, period or region, and click + on the ones you like. Then press Add to collection.
  2. Curator. Press Start. Masterpiece puts a painting on your desktop, fitted to each screen, and changes it as often as you like.
  3. Let it be personal. Choose Curator chooses and set your taste with the sliders: every day the Curator adds a few new paintings it thinks you'll love.

Paintings are saved in Pictures\Masterpiece. Pause or stop any time on the Curator tab; Restore my wallpaper puts your own wallpaper back.
An independent open-source project, not an official app of any museum.
